This site is a drawer. I put pictures in it, and sentences, and the odd scrap. I do not label the drawer very well on purpose.

I like having a place that is not for anyone in particular. If you are reading this, you found it, which is fine. There is no argument I am trying to win here, and no version of myself I am trying to present. Only the things I did not want to lose.

Some of them are placeholders. The real ones will replace them when they exist. That feels honest enough.
