![Prosecco and charcuterie at sunset](/writing/same-sky.jpg)

A delicious prosecco on the first hot day of summer,  
our hands touch as we both reach for the charcuterie board and you blush,  
neither of us quite ready to let the moment pass.  

The air is warm and skies are blue as the clouds smile upon us together.  
and for a heartbeat, everything else fades into golden light.  

Inside our golden light the rest of the world fades away.  
And in that glow, a supersonic boom is heard.  
Forever begins for us.  

The shockwave still ringing, two hearts now flying the same sky.
