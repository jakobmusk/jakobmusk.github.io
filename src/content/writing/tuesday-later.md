The light in this room changes its mind around four. I sat with it until it decided.

Nothing happened. A truck somewhere. The usual sounds of a building that is not empty and not full. I made a note of the color on the wall — not quite yellow, not quite anything — and then I did not make a note of anything else.

I used to think a day needed a shape. I am less convinced.
